The Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world. It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India, on the banks of the Yamuna River. Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan built it in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal.

The monument is made of white marble and is famous for its elegant design, symmetry, and detailed carvings. Precious and semi-precious stones are inlaid into the marble, especially around the entrance and tomb. Beautiful gardens, fountains, and reflecting pools surround the central mausoleum.

Construction of the Taj Mahal began in 1632 and took about 22 years to complete. Thousands of skilled workers and artisans contributed to the project. Its combination of Mughal, Persian, and Indian architectural styles makes it unique.

Today, the Taj Mahal is a symbol of love and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Millions of tourists visit it every year. It remains an important part of India’s history and cultural heritage.
